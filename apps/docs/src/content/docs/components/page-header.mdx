The title of a page, with its context and actions.

## Usage

Use it once, at the top of a screen.

## Variants

- With an **eyebrow**, a **subtitle** and **actions**, or the title alone.

## States

- Static.

## Dimensions

Title in `--si-font-size-3xl`.

## Accessibility

- It holds the only `<h1>` of the page.

## Responsive

The actions move under the title when there is no room.

<Examples component="page-header" />

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Fragment slot="do">Keep the main action on the right, and the title short.</Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="dont">Do not fill it with more than a few actions: use a [menu](/components/menu/).</Fragment>
</DoDont>

## API

Partial `@si/page-header.html.twig`: `title`, `eyebrow`, `subtitle`, block `actions`.
