Moves through the pages of a long list.

## Usage

Use it under a table or a list split into pages.

## Variants

- Numbered links, with previous and next.

## States

- `aria-current="page"` for the current page, `aria-disabled="true"` at the ends.

## Dimensions

Targets of at least 32px.

## Accessibility

- It sits in a `<nav>` with an `aria-label`.
- The current page is carried by `aria-current`, not only by its colour.

## Responsive

The links wrap on a narrow screen.
