A key figure with its label.

## Usage

Use a row of KPIs at the top of a dashboard or a list.

## Variants

- **Tones** on the icon: `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info`.
- **Link** (`href`) or **filter** (`pressed`).
- `si-kpi-row` for a row.

## States

- A filter uses `aria-pressed`. Links and filters have hover and `:focus-visible` states.

## Dimensions

The row gives each KPI at least 150px.

## Accessibility

- The label says what the figure means: the tone only supports it.
- A KPI that acts is a real link or button.

## Responsive

`si-kpi-row` wraps by itself.
