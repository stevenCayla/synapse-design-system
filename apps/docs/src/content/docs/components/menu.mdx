A list of actions behind a button.

## Usage

Use it for the secondary actions of a row or a page.

## Variants

- Links and buttons, a **divider** (`si-menu__divider`) and a **danger** item (`si-menu__item--danger`).

## States

- It opens under its button, or above when there is no room. A disabled item is skipped.

## Dimensions

At least 180px wide, with items of 32px.

## Accessibility

- A native popover: Escape and a click outside close it, and the focus returns to the button.
- The focus moves to the first item, and arrows move through the items.
- It is a list of links and buttons, without the `menu` role.

## Responsive

It stays inside the screen.

## Do and don't

<DoDont>
  <Fragment slot="do">Put the destructive action last, after a divider.</Fragment>
  <Fragment slot="dont">Do not hide the main action of a page in it.</Fragment>
</DoDont>

## API

No partial. Button: `popovertarget`. Menu: `popover`, `data-si-menu`. Classes: `si-menu`, `__list`, `__item`, `__item--danger`, `__divider`. Needs the behaviours script: `import '.../js/behaviors.js'`.
