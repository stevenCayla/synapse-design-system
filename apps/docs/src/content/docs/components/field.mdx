A form control with its label, help and error.

## Usage

Use it for every form input. The partial adds the label, the help and the error; the control stays yours, so any input fits.

## Variants

- **Input, select, textarea:** `si-input` on the native element.
- **Checkbox and radio:** `si-check`, grouped in `si-fieldset` with a `<legend>`.
- **Switch:** `si-check__input--switch` with `role="switch"`, for a setting that applies at once.
- **Input group:** `si-input-group`, with a text or a button.
- **Drop zone:** `si-dropzone`, around a real file input.

## States

- **Required:** `required` on the control, and `required: true` for the mark.
- **Invalid:** `aria-invalid="true"`, with the error linked by `aria-describedby`.
- **Disabled** and **read-only:** the native attributes.

## Dimensions

A control is 36px high, or 32px with `si-input--sm`. Check marks are 16px, in a label that is the click target.

## Accessibility

- Every control has a visible label, linked by `for` and `id`.
- An error says what to do, and is linked to the control.
- The required mark is not enough on its own: pass `required_label`.
- A group of options goes in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`.

## Responsive

Controls fill their container. Lay out several fields with `si-stack` or your own grid.

## API

Partial `@si/field.html.twig`: `id`, `label`, `required`, `required_label`, `hint`, `error`, block `control`. A Symfony form theme comes with the migration of the application.
