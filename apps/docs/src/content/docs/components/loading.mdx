Shows that content is on its way.

## Usage

Use the skeleton when the layout of the content is known, and the spinner otherwise. In a button, see [button](/components/button/).

## Variants

- **Spinner with a label:** the partial.
- **Spinner alone:** `si-spinner`, `si-spinner--sm`.
- **Skeleton:** `si-skeleton--text`, `si-skeleton--block`.

## States

- Animated, and still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Dimensions

Spinner of 20px, or 14px in small. A skeleton takes the size of its container.

## Accessibility

- `role="status"` announces the wait: give it a name.
- A decorative spinner has `aria-hidden="true"`.

## Responsive

It follows its container.
