Groups the content about one subject.

## Usage

Use a card for an item in a grid, or a block of related content. A plain section with a heading is often enough.

## Variants

- With an **icon**, **subtitle** and **figures** (`stats`).
- **Accent** (`accent: true`): a coloured top edge, set by `--si-card-accent`.
- **Footer** (block `footer`) for actions.
- **Clickable** (`href`): the whole card is a link.

## States

- A clickable card has hover and `:focus-visible` states.

## Dimensions

It fills its container. Spacing comes from the tokens.

## Accessibility

- Choose the heading level with `level`, to keep the order of the page.
- A clickable card contains no other link or button.
- An accent colour is decorative.

## Responsive

Lay out cards with your own grid. Figures wrap.
